# AI correctness audit (2026-11-24)

**Scope:** the 11 AI features in `backend/ai_providers/features.py`:
- chat and Advanced chat;
- weekly and monthly insights, insight translation and custom insights;
- budget suggestions and budget auto-create;
- entries from text, a receipt photo and voice.

**How it was done:** a code-only review (no live model calls) of what each feature gives the model, what it asks it, and what happens to the answer. The most serious findings were then checked again by hand.

**Status:**
- **Verified by hand:** confirmed again in the code during this audit.
- **Reviewed:** found with line evidence in the review, not re-checked by hand.

## Already right

- **Nothing is saved without the user:** no AI-created entry is saved until the user taps Save, and an entry with no amount can't be saved.
- **Chat:**
  - It has no tools, so it can only reply with text.
  - It uses the right book and the asker's own "today".
  - Transfers between the personal and family wallets are left out of income and spending.
- **Custom insight:**
  - Its figures are computed by code.
  - It is told not to invent or recompute numbers.
  - The user's instruction is quoted, and treated as a request, not as rules for the model.
  - A failed or refused report gives the use back.
- **Provider layer:** refusals and empty answers move on to the backup provider, and categories are always matched to the real catalogue.

## Group 2: instructions that invite invented content (need a live test)

- **Weekly and monthly insight prompts** (`insights_service.py:543-621`, `:1214-1306`):
  - There is no "use only the given numbers" rule.
  - They ask for goal contributions, projected dates, a "Financial Health Score" and multi-week comparisons the model never gets.
  - They require 800–1500 words whatever the data, at temperature 0.7.
  - The previous report's first 500 characters are passed as its "recommendations".
- **Chat** (`ai_chatbot.py:1040-1183`):
  - It claims "complete access" while the model sees only 6–12 search results.
  - The question-type keywords are English only.
  - There is no "say you don't have it" rule and no off-topic rule.
  - Transaction descriptions are not marked as data. In a family book they come from other members.
  - Overlapping budgets are summed into one total.
  - "Average transaction" mixes income and spending.
  - Old history is sent without dates.
- **Budget suggestions** (`budget_service.py:424-486`):
  - They ask for sub-category budgets without sub-category data.
  - The income rule applies even with no income.
  - The model is asked for fields it can only invent.
  - `user_context` is unbounded and sits in the system prompt.
  - The reasoning is English only.
  - The rule-based fallback is labelled "AI analysis".
- **Entry extraction** (`transaction_routes.py:942-977`, `:1034-1083`, `:1292-1327`):
  - There is no "never guess" rule.
  - There are no rules for Myanmar numerals and units (၀-၉, ထောင်, သောင်း, သိန်း, lakh).
  - "5k" may become K5.
  - Which receipt amount counts as the total isn't defined (cash tendered, change).
  - The kyat word starts with a Thai letter.
  - The prompt has no weekday for relative dates.
  - A missing `type` becomes outflow.
- **Translation** (`insights_service.py:849-871`): the output isn't checked (numbers, currency symbols, script).
- **Everywhere:** nothing checks that the numbers in the model's text exist in the given figures.

## Group 3: reliability and UX

- **Cut-off answers:** chat answers and weekly or monthly reports cut off at the token limit are saved as complete. The custom insight accepts a cut-off last section.
- **Failed runs:** a failed Monday or 1st-of-month run is never retried, so last period's report stays up. The app doesn't show which period a report covers.
- **Failed translation:** it turns `GET /api/insights?language=mm` into a 503, even though the English report exists.
- **Review screens:** only the amount can be edited. Type, category, date, currency and description can only be accepted or discarded. For whom is never set.
- **Receipt images:** they are fitted into 1024×1024, so long receipts become hard to read, and EXIF rotation is ignored.
- **Voice:** whisper isn't asked for speech detection, so filler transcripts ("Thank you.") can be used. The transcript can't be edited.
- **Structured output:** a strict JSON schema is sent only to Claude; OpenAI and Gemini get plain JSON mode.
- **Default model:** `gemini-2.5-flash`, the default for chat and insights, is routed by Google to a different model from 2026-12-01.
- **Lengths:** chat messages and history have no length limit.
- **Raw errors:** extraction errors show raw exception text.
